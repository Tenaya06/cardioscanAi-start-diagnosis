What is CardioScanAI?

CardioScanAI is a revolutionary multi-modal diagnostic platform. Unlike traditional invasive coronary angiography, our system leverages the power of advanced deep learning to provide a safer, faster, and more accessible alternative.

CT Analysis

Detailed extraction of structural narrowing and plaque composition from CT angiography slices.

ECG Signals

Real-time processing of electrical anomalies and rhythm patterns to detect ischemia.

MRI Fusion
